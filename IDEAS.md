# Six ideas (procedural pixel art, seeded by the front pages)

1. **Almost Everywhere** — *"Are we dead almost everywhere?"* + measure theory.
   Render the **nodal set** (zero-contours) of a sum of random plane waves
   (Berry's random-wave model — the universal stuff of quantum chaos). The
   field is one sign almost everywhere; *life* is the measure-zero curve where
   it crosses zero. Glowing filaments on a vast dark void.

2. **The Incompleteness Tile** — Gödel, appearing on *both* front pages.
   An aperiodic (Penrose-like) tiling carrying one unresolvable defect that
   refuses to close — a system that cannot complete itself.

3. **Doubly Periodic** — elliptic integrals / special functions.
   Domain-coloring of the **Weierstrass ℘ function** over its lattice. A field
   of poles and zeros that tiles the plane in two directions at once. *(The
   FANCY 4096×4096 centerpiece.)*

4. **Natural Boundary** — *"is f holomorphic?"* / analytic continuation.
   A lacunary series (Σ z^(2^n)) whose unit circle is a wall it can never be
   continued past. A function imprisoned inside its own domain.

5. **Deterministic Freedom** — *"no randomness… what is freedom?"*
   Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion from a fixed, seed-less initial condition.
   A wholly deterministic rule that nonetheless grows organic, unrepeatable,
   "free"-looking life.

6. **Minimum Cover** — *"minimum number of sets."*
   The plane greedily covered by overlapping discs; color = covering depth.
   A picture of how few sets it takes to leave nothing uncovered.

## Chosen three (max conceptual + visual diversity)
- **#1 Almost Everywhere** — sparse measure-zero glow (random-wave nodal lines)
- **#3 Doubly Periodic** — domain-colored Weierstrass ℘ — the 4096² centerpiece
- **#5 Deterministic Freedom** — Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion
