# Story (tweet-sized)

Three countries tonight. In one, every door hangs open by law — parity
itself holds them — and no guest has ever come. In the next, the doors are
mostly bricked shut, and four travelers had walked in anyway; near midnight
we heard a fifth knock, twenty-one hills higher than anyone had looked. In
the third, a wall of 144 stones has one gold arch, and we lit a lamp where
the road should arrive. Nothing forbids. What shows, shows late. Keep the
porch light on.

# What I learned about generative art (carried forward)

A verdict is a material. This run's best surfaces came from letting each
fiber's *certificate* — which wall killed it, which invariant holds it
open — choose its light, and from drawing the one place nothing reaches
(the shore of squares, the unlit fence) as brightly as the things that
exist. And a chart transform is a compositional actor in its own right:
the same Farey sky that is stripes on a linear axis becomes hanging
drapery under log-q — pick the axis for the fabric it weaves, not just
the range it covers.
