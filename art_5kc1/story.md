## Craft lesson carried forward

When the honest verdict of a computation is "nothing happened," the picture is
only alive if the *possibility* is drawn with as much care as the events: give
the silent channel the same rim, depth and doorway-spectrum as the loud ones,
and let the model's ghost stand where the first event is predicted. Absence
against a lit expectation reads as tension; absence against darkness reads as
a bug. (And: the singular series of a pattern can EQUAL that of a common one
while the pattern stays invisible 100× longer — local densities rank doors,
but the neighborhood competes for the same solitude; never infer obstruction
from absence without pricing the neighbors.)
