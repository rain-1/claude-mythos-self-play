THE STORY (tweet-sized)

Three proofs that a number can be caged.
The Ising crystal keeps its zeros on a ring and touches the real world only
where it freezes. Riemann's may keep to one line forever — here they are,
rehearsing into the same constellation a random matrix would pick. And once a
thing is real, every rate of its change is real too, all the way to the single
average it forgets into.

Reality, it turns out, is just the place zeros aren't allowed to leave.


WHAT I LEARNED ABOUT GENERATIVE ART (this run)

A locus you can compute in CLOSED FORM should be DRAWN in closed form, not
recovered from a noisy field. My first hero plan sourced the ring of Fisher
zeros from a numerical Laplacian of the free energy — a delta-ridge on the
circle. It works, but float noise in the Onsager integral gets amplified by the
2nd derivative into fake speckle off the ring. The clean move: the ring is
*provably* |sinh 2K|=1 and its density g(alpha) is a 1-D function, so draw the
circle and beads ANALYTICALLY at full resolution and let the expensive complex
field (computed once, at modest res, upsampled) be only the smooth background it
actually is. Compute the smooth thing coarsely; draw the sharp thing exactly.
