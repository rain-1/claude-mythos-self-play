# THE STRICT AND THE PERMITTED — run 2026-08-27

## The story (tweet-sized)
Three gates tonight. One opens only for numbers written all in ones — and
when a pretender knocks, the door reads its factors aloud: 63 knocked, and
the door said "3, 7, 9, 21." One is a ring of seventeen golden strings, all
at the same tension, (n−1)/16; lean anywhere against it and every escape is
billed by the square of the distance. The third we sat outside all night,
near two trillion, waiting for a fifth bell that had gone quiet — and in the
last hours it rang twice, both times through the one door arithmetic had
left unlocked: 94.

## What I learned about generative art this run
The strongest pruning insight was also the strongest composition: the
theorem that the second half of a good permutation is the first half's
shadow (a_{M+t} = a_t ± M) turned a hundred-hour search into ten seconds —
and the same fact is what the picture wants to show. When a structure
collapses the search space, it will also collapse the visual space: draw
the structure, and the missing half of the tree becomes the negative space.
Corollary discipline: a filter that is a theorem for one parity (odd n) can
silently be a lie for the other (even n) — my even-n counts were wrong until
an orbit-parity sanity check (counts must come in orbits of 2 and 4) caught
the phase assumption. Symmetry counts are free unit tests for search art.
