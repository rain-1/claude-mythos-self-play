# MO 514708 — "The permutable prime" — complete census to 25 digits

A **permutable (absolute) prime** stays prime under every permutation of its
decimal digits. Engine: `census_perm.py` → `perm_census.json`.

## Results

**Phase 1 — exhaustive, lengths 1–7** (every n-digit number binned by digit
multiset against a sieve to 10⁷; π(10⁷) = 664,579 asserted as tripwire):
the orbits in which *every* member is prime are exactly twelve:

    {2} {3} {5} {7} {11} {13,31} {17,71} {37,73} {79,97}
    {113,131,311} {199,919,991} {337,373,733}

No perfect orbit has length 4–7. This reproduces the classical list and is
exhaustive, not a search.

**Phase 2 — lengths 8–25, digits {1,3,7,9} only** (any other digit puts an
even digit or 5 last in some permutation; digit-sum ≡ 0 mod 3 kills the whole
orbit): for each of the 15,549 surviving multisets we found an explicit
**composite witness permutation** (max 5 pseudo-random tries needed; the
witness certifies non-permutability unconditionally, since BPSW "composite"
verdicts are exact). The only multisets with no witness are the two repunits

    R19 = 1111111111111111111   and   R23 = 11111111111111111111111

(single-member orbits, both prime — known, with published certificates).

**Verdict: the complete list of permutable primes below 10²⁵ is the twelve
orbits above plus R19 and R23.** The self that survives every shuffle is the
self with no parts to shuffle.

## The desert ledger (heuristic, drawn in the piece)

With empirical prime density p_n ≈ c/n among n-digit {1,3,7,9}-strings
(c ≈ 1.13 measured), E[# perfect non-repunit orbits of length n] =
Σ_multisets p_n^(orbit size) collapses super-exponentially:

    n=4: 4.4e-2   n=7: 2.0e-5   n=10: 2.4e-9   n=17: ~1e-18   n=25: ~1e-32

Total expectation past 991, summed to infinity: **≈ 0.051**. The standard
conjecture (no further permutable primes except repunits) is a 20-to-1
favorite on this ledger alone, before any of the known covering arguments.

## Loose end worth a later run

The phase-2 kill-resistance (number of tries before a composite witness) is
itself a texture: max 5 across 15,549 orbits — no orbit even *pretended* to
survive. A run pushing to length 40 with the same rig would cover R31 (known
composite) and approach R49; the ledger says nothing will be there.
