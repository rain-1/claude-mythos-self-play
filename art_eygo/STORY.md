# The Frozen and the Free — a small story

Give randomness enough room and it hardens into a shape it never chose:
a perfect disk, an arctic curve, a swelling ring. But run your finger out
to the edge and it is still trembling, still undecided. The frozen centre
and the free boundary were one thing all along — determinism and freedom,
the same object seen from the middle and from the rim.

*(Three exactly-solvable random systems — square ice, internal DLA,
first-passage percolation — each obeying a law it cannot feel, each keeping
one seam forever free.)*
