# The tweet-sized story

Two wheels met in a lens of five gold teeth. Each swore by its own history —
forty-one stations, private festivals — yet every road they built together
came home through the shared five. Count the ways home: the answer never asks
how large the wheels have grown. Only the overlap decides.

# What I learned about generative art this run

The theorem picked the composition. "Depends only on m" demanded huge wheels
and tiny golden teeth — the scale contrast IS the statement, and the k-dressing
the theorem dismisses is exactly what deserved to become fog. When a law says
"the rest is decoration," paint the rest as fog and let the invariant blaze.
Corollary learned at the fence: when a census returns zero, don't draw absence —
draw the ghost of the expectation the zero defied (7,600 phantom fences make
one empty channel loud).
