## Theme: **WHAT THE MOTION KEEPS**
Conservation as memory. Each panel is a system in violent motion carrying an exact
invariant: the invariant is the memory, the motion is the forgetting.

## The six

1. **The Sky of Returns** (HERO, 4096²) — Boole map T(x) = x − 1/x. Preserves Lebesgue
   measure on ℝ (Boole 1857) yet the invariant measure is INFINITE: conservative +
   ergodic (Adler–Weiss), so a.e. orbit returns to every window infinitely often while
   spending almost all its time on enormous excursions. During an excursion x² drops by
   exactly 2−1/x² ≈ 2 per step, so height² is a clock. Render an ensemble of orbits as
   comet arcs in (time, sign(x)·height): launched from Nothing (x≈0) to Infinity
   (x≈1/x₀), sailing back down, re-launched on the other side. VERIFY: Boole's integral
   identity (measure preservation on windows) + Lamperti/Thaler arcsine law for the
   fraction of time spent positive (excursion durations are stable-½).
   Phil hook: Infinity and Nothing as the two ends of one breath; measure conserved =
   memory conserved, yet never at home.

2. **Leapfrog / The Braid of Rings** — two-or-three coaxial thin-core vortex rings
   (Dyson's model, mutual induction via elliptic integrals K,E). They leapfrog: each
   ring slides through the other, trading radius for speed, forever. Total hydrodynamic
   impulse P = π Σ Γᵢ Rᵢ² is EXACTLY conserved through the exchange. Each pass-through
   is literally a generator of the LOOP BRAID GROUP (motion group of circles in ℝ³ —
   live MO seed). Render the spacetime worldtubes in comoving coordinates: two/three
   glowing corkscrew tubes threading each other. VERIFY: impulse + energy conservation,
   leapfrog period against literature.

3. **The Families of Loss** — the subtract-a-prime-divisor game, straight from today's
   MO front page. L(0)=L(1)=lose; move n → n−p, p a prime divisor. Compute the full
   win/loss table to ~2×10⁸ by sieve. The losing positions organize into families:
   2p, 4p (thin, prime-like density), 2^k (a geometric ladder), the odd squares-ish
   sporadics, and a WILD set (114 elements to 2^29 — smooth, structureless, the game's
   true irregularity). Render the loss set as a radial constellation, family = hue,
   wild = ember heroes; N-positions as near-black ground with optimal-move drainage
   filaments flowing INTO the losses (every winning move lands on a loss — the losses
   are the attractors of perfect play). VERIFY: reproduce the MO poster's loss list
   prefix + wild census.
