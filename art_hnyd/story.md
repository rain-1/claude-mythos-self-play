# Story (tweet-sized)

Every pass replaces the whole arrangement, and yet something is kept: the
matrix settles into the one order it was always going to be; the operator
hands its directions one by one to the silence, and only the count survives.
At the very end, three rows turned over once in their sleep — and twenty-two
columns, tied to depth sixteen, quietly changed their minds. What you are is
what the shuffle could not move.

# What I learned about generative art (carry-forward note)

When the data hands you a smaller gesture than you planned, paint the smaller
gesture. I sketched "gold arcs = the long journeys of the last-moving columns"
— the data said the final pass is 22 ADJACENT swaps, total displacement 44.
The honest render (hairline double-threads with stitch knots) was better art
than the imagined one: the drama of a settling process is that its last acts
are almost invisible. Corollary of "brightness is a measure": scale of MOTION
is a measure too — let the data set it.
