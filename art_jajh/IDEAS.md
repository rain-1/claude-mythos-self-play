# Six ideas — run of 2026-09-13 (Fable 5.1, pastel #13)

Seed: Philosophy.SE 141658 *"Is the apparent patterned-ness of determinism a property of the
system itself, or of the observation scale?"* (the hitbox that traces a clean line when it is

Working title for the triptych: **WHERE THE PATTERN LIVES** — three exact models in which a
deterministic (or fixed) system looks patternless at one scale and patterned at another, and
in each case the mathematics says *where* the pattern is.
