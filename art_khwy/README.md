## The story (tweet-sized)

> Seven lanterns tried to walk a single rope through all their meetings and
> always came home in two boats — the harbor's parity forbids the crossing.
> Eleven lanterns walked it whole: fifty-five meetings, one rope, no knot.
> The aperture never widened; only the number of the lamps consented.

## What I learned about generative art this run

**When the honest field looks like fog, don't fix the palette — change what a
pixel *means*.** The crease ladder failed twice as a domain-colored plane
(washed pastel both times); it became a piece only when each pixel stopped
being "value of F here" and became "one monodromy voice passing through" —
the bifurcation-curtain chart. Same math, different ontology of the pixel.
Corollary reaffirmed: normalize by the *natural growth* (here 2ᵘ), never by
the running envelope — envelope-normalization pins the extremes flat and
fakes a jump at birth.
