## The six ideas

1. **The Nursery of Numbers** — Conway's surreal numbers as a birthday
   cascade. Every number is born from two sets of earlier numbers, and on day
   zero there is nothing at all: 0 = { | }. Render the sign-expansion tree,
   day 0 at the top, days converging geometrically to the **day-ω shoreline**
   where the real continuum suddenly appears — with famous latecomers (1/3,
   √2−1, e−2, π−3, φ−1) as glowing threads that only reach the shore at ω,
   while every dyadic rational lands early. Seeds: MO surreals question +
   Phil.SE solipsism ("a universe generated from a single act").
   → HERO CANDIDATE, 4096².

2. **Chord Genesis** — the elliptic curve of the Diophantine triple {1,3,8}:
   y² = (x+1)(3x+1)(8x+1). Fermat's point (120, 6479) extends the triple to a
   quadruple; the group law generates infinitely many rational points from
   finitely many. Draw the *secant construction itself*: thousands of chords
   between orbit points, additively — the curve emerges as the caustic
   envelope of its own arithmetic. Brightness = equidistribution measure
   dx/2y. Seed: MO Diophantine sextuples question.

3. **One Cell Speaks** — Rule 110 (universal!) grown from a single living
   cell. Detect the periodic ether and light only the *deviations* — gliders
   as messengers carrying computation. Seed: Leibniz characteristica
   universalis (one calculus that can say everything) + open Rule-110 seed
   from the memory branch.

4. **The Colored Knot** — Fox p-colorings / quandle structure of a large
   random alternating knot, rendered as glossy woven rope with arcs lit by
   color class. Seed: MO computable-quandle question + open quandle seed.

5. **The Zombie's Twin** — Arnold cat map Poincaré recurrence: a portrait
   dissolves into statistical noise and then, at the exact recurrence period,
   reassembles. Which frame is the zombie? Seed: Phil.SE "Are some people
   zombies?".

6. **Müntz's Gap** — two fans of monomial curves x^λ: one family with
   Σ 1/λ = ∞ that can approximate any continuous function, one with Σ 1/λ < ∞
   that leaves a permanently unreachable gap. The reachable and the
   unreachable rendered as light vs shadow. Seed: MO Müntz–Szász question.
