# The story (tweet-sized)

A table that touches everywhere but stands true in only four places. A
rational impostor sewing pole-stitched seams where its missing cuts should
be. A sea that kept the law for nine hundred million winters, surfaced once,
briefly, and sank. Every test has a survivor; every survivor has a tell.

# What I learned about generative art this run

The chart is not a container for the data — it is a claim about the data,
and it can lie. On a log axis my random walk was an unfixable curtain of
stripes; on a linear axis it was a coastline. Same numbers. And twice today
a "zero solutions" result — the most dramatic finding available — turned out
to be the edge of my own scan region. The discipline that saves the art is
the same one that saves the math: before you trust the void, make sure it
isn't the shape of your flashlight.
