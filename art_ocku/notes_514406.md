# MO 514406 — Chameleon population dynamics: exact verification notes

**Question** (38 pts, active): cyclic chameleons — R+B→RR, B+G→BB, G+R→GG,
uniformly random meetings, start (1 red, 1 blue, N green). Conjectured
(updated by the poster):

- N·P(red wins) → 2π/(3√3)          = 1.2091995761…
- N·P(green wins) → ½ + ln3 − π/(3√3) = 0.9940125006…
- N·(1−P(blue wins)) → ½ + ln3 + π/(3√3) = 2.2032120767…

## Method
Exact absorbing-chain linear algebra on the whole simplex {(r,b,g): r+b+g=M}.
Effective transitions (null meetings dropped): weights rb → (r+1,b−1,g),
bg → (r,b+1,g−1), gr → (r−1,b,g+1). Boundary verdicts: b=0 ⇒ green wins;
g=0 ⇒ red wins; r=0 ⇒ blue wins. One sparse LU per M solves all three
fields on every interior state (`chameleon.py`, `solve_hero.py`).

## Verification chain
- Solver vs Monte-Carlo at (1,1,10) and (3,4,5): agreement within MC error
  (200k trials each) — `cham_mc.py`.
- C₃ symmetry P_R(r,b,g) = P_B(g,r,b): max error 4.9e−14 across 200 random
  states at M=400 (exactly the LU noise floor) — `preview3.py`.
- Iterative-refinement certificate at M=640: max correction 5.5e−14,
  far below the smallest interior deviation 7.5e−11 — the field is signal.
- Ladder of corner values N = 50 … 3000 (`ladder.txt`, `ladder2.txt`).

## Results
Fit N·P = c₀ + c₁·lnN/N + c₂/N on N ≥ 400 (holdout: fitting N ≤ 1200
predicts the N=2046 value to 1.6e−6):

- N·P_R → **1.2092033**  vs conjectured 1.2091996  (Δ ≈ 3.7e−6)
- N·P_G → **0.9940320**  vs conjectured 0.9940125  (Δ ≈ 2.0e−5)

Both constants confirmed to ~5 significant digits. The lnN/N correction
term is real (pure 1/N fits are visibly worse).

## Additional structure (not in the question)
- **Bulk amnesia**: from any interior state with all three populations
  macroscopic, the three win probabilities are equal to within a deviation
  that decays exponentially with M (measured floors: 9.2e−10 at M=512,
  7.5e−11 at M=640, 2.2e−13 at M=768). All decision lives in a boundary
  skin; the deviation field forms a chiral three-armed spiral (the hero
  render).
- **Chirality**: the rules are invariant under the 3-cycle (RBG) but not
  under transpositions; measured: max |P_R(b,r,g) − P_B(r,b,g)| = 0.99 —
  swapping two colours reverses destiny almost completely.
- Near each pure-population corner the almost-sure winner is that colour's
  predator (e.g. (N,1,1): green wins a.s.) — the corner (1,1,N) of the
  question is the g-corner instance of this.

## Finite-size law (observed conjecture, this run)
The ladder is fit extremely well (holdout-validated) by
  N·P_R(1,1,N) = 2π/3√3 − c₁·lnN/N + c₂/N,  c₁ ≈ 3.76 ± 0.1
  N·P_G(1,1,N) = (½+ln3−π/3√3) − c₁'·lnN/N + c₂'/N,  c₁' ≈ 1.45 ± 0.05
The lnN/N term is required (pure 1/N fits fail the holdout). Numerology
note, flagged as such: c₁ ≈ π·(2π/3√3) = 3.799 within fit error.
