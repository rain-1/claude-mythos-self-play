### 1. **The Far Country** — hyperbolic {p,q} kaleidoscope (Poincaré disk)  ★ centerpiece
Per-pixel fold-into-fundamental-domain in the Poincaré disk: for each point invert
across the three mirror circles of a Schwarz triangle (angles π/p, π/q, π/2) until
it lands inside the fundamental triangle, counting reflections. Color by reflection
count (concentric word-length rings = a Cayley-graph mandala) and by position in
the fundamental triangle (faceting). The ideal boundary |z|=1 is a **singularity**
where tiles shrink to zero — infinite multiscale crowding that genuinely rewards
4096² (craft note: "singularities are free detail"). Seeds: logic-as-group
(syllogism questions) + *emergent monadology* (a whole world inside a finite disk).
NEW technique. → **4096² hero.**

### 2. **The Presence of Absence** — Eisenstein primes in the plane
The Eisenstein integers a+bω (ω = e^{iπ/3}) tile ℂ in a triangular lattice with
6-fold symmetry. Plot the *primes* among them — but tone-map so the eye reads the
piece through its **voids**: the prime-free corridors, the holes left by the
units and ramified prime 3. Presence rendered so that absence is what you see
(philosophy front page). NEW technique (Gaussian/Eisenstein primes were an unbuilt
vein). → 2048².

### 3. **Phenomenal Red** — the chromaticity horseshoe & metameric black
Integrate monochromatic and mixed spectral power distributions against the CIE 1931
color-matching functions; render the spectral locus (the "horseshoe"), the line of
purples, and the gamut of real color — bounded by the region of *imaginary* colors
no spectrum can reach (the explanatory gap made geometric). Metamers = the many
spectra mapping to one point. Directly answers "the phenomenal experience of red."
The one genuinely **color-science** piece — smooth gamut against the others' line
and point fields. NEW technique. → 2048².
