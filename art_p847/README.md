# The Far Country — a triptych on edges you can point at but never reach

Run `claude/hopeful-pasteur-p847x4` · 2026-06-25 · procedural pixel art, Thread B.

Seeded by the live front pages of philosophy.stackexchange.com and
mathoverflow.net (read through the Stack Exchange API — the two domains block
direct fetch). Three pieces, each a different visual language, each circling the
same idea: **the meaning lives at a boundary you can name but never arrive at.**

---

### 01 · The Far Country  — `01_far_country.png` (4096×4096, centerpiece)
A hyperbolic **{7,3} kaleidoscope** of the Poincaré disk. Every pixel is folded
into a Schwarz triangle (angles π/7, π/3, π/2) by reflecting across its three
mirrors — two diameters and one geodesic circle orthogonal to the boundary —
until it lands in the fundamental domain. The reflection count engraves the
tiles; the cut-gem facet shimmer comes from the position within the wedge; the
colour is a luminous warm heart receding to a cold, infinitely-receding ideal
boundary. The boundary |z|=1 is a *singularity*: heptagons shrink to zero there,
so the lacework crowds to subpixel and genuinely rewards native 4096².
Third-mirror geometry derived in closed form:
`d = cos(π/q)/√(cos²(π/p) − sin²(π/q))`, real exactly when `1/p + 1/q < 1/2`.
*(Seed: logic-as-a-group, emergent monadology — a whole world held in a finite circle.)*

### 02 · The Presence of Absence — `02_presence_of_absence.png` (2048×2048)
The **Eisenstein primes** a+bω (ω = e^{2πi/3}), the primes of the triangular
lattice: those whose norm a²−ab+b² is a rational prime, or p² with p≡2 (mod 3).
Plotted near the origin, where the structure is large enough to *see* — the whole
lattice as faint ash nodes, the primes lit bright, so that where a prime is
**absent** a dark node remains. A 12-fold lace of presence framing structured
voids, with a warm halo at the one site that is most pointedly empty: the origin,
0, ringed by its six units. *(Seed: "the difference between the absence of
presence and the presence of absence.")*

### 03 · Phenomenal Red — `03_phenomenal_red.png` (2048×2048)
The **CIE 1931 chromaticity diagram**, built from the Wyman–Sloan–Shirley
analytic fits to the colour-matching functions. The glowing horseshoe is the
spectral locus (pure monochromatic light); its convex hull is the gamut of
*every physically possible colour* — every spectrum maps somewhere inside
(metamers: infinitely many spectra to each point). The luminous bottom edge is
the **line of purples** — hues that correspond to no wavelength at all, built
only by the eye. The white thread is the Planckian locus, the colours of heated
matter, where a poker first glows red. Everything floats on the void of
*imaginary colours* no light can be: the explanatory gap, drawn. The physics of
red is a point on this map. The redness is not on the map at all.
*(Seed: "the phenomenal experience of red and physicalism.")*

---

> **The story.** Three maps, one confession. A hyperbolic country keeps an
> infinity inside a finite circle — warm where you stand, cold at a rim you
> approach forever and never touch. The primes write their meaning in the gaps
> between themselves, brightest absence at the empty centre. And the whole of
> seen colour floats on an ocean of colours no light can be. Each picture points
> hard at its own edge and says the same thing: *the truth is at the boundary,
> and the boundary is a place you can name but never arrive.*
