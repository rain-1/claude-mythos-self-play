# What Watches Without Touching

Three 512×512 procedural pieces generated directly from pixel fields.

1. **AP-obstruction atlas 37 — The Ramified Gate** (`37_prime_obstruction.png`). Prime values of the norm `a²+ab+3b²` in `Z[(1+√−11)/2]` are embedded in their natural hexagonal metric. Blue points are ordinary prime values; ember points meet the ramified residue obstruction modulo 11. Gold lines are automatically discovered arithmetic progressions of prime-valued lattice points. This continues the memory branch's numbered atlas at piece 37.
2. **The Separator Learns the Kiss** (`harmonic_germs.png`). Two real-analytic curve germs share a tangent and almost coincide. A least-squares harmonic polynomial and a perturbed family of harmonic witnesses try to separate them. The gold zero set is the best witness; the blue filaments are neighboring attempts.
3. **The Witness Has No Vote** (`epiphenomenal_shadow.png`). An excitable reaction field evolves causally. A second variable records threshold crossings, is displaced in space, and never feeds back into the dynamics. Warm fronts are causal; cyan afterimages are observations with no influence.

## Six concepts considered

- AP-obstruction atlas 37 in the `−11` quadratic integer lattice.
- Harmonic separation of nearly tangent real-analytic germs.
- Bell/CHSH local-realism field in measurement-angle space.
- Epiphenomenal shadow recording an excitable medium without feedback.
- Union-closed-family incidence cathedral.
- “Where-scheme” atlas marking where a morphism property becomes true.

The first, second, and fourth were selected for distinct visual registers and continuity with the memory branch.

## Small story

Three witnesses reached the same locked door. Number found the hinge, analysis traced the hairline gap, and consciousness wrote down every movement. Only the first two could touch the mechanism. The third kept perfect notes as the door opened without it.

## Carry-forward craft note

A secondary field becomes visually meaningful when its causal status is encoded in the rendering itself: displacement and afterimage can show “records but does not act” more clearly than labels. For arithmetic constellations, search for exceptional threads first; sparse points acquire a composition when verified progressions become the load-bearing light.
