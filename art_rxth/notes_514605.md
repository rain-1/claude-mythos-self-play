# MO 514605 — the 4/3 spacing of Collatz record breakers: what the data say

Question (2026-08, fresh, unanswered): consecutive ratios of A006877
(delay record breakers) have median ≈ 4/3; is lim R_{k+1}/R_k = 4/3 known /
plausible?

`collatz.c`: memoized total-stopping-time engine (2^30-entry tables, u128

and the poster's shortcut. Verified:

- the full-map and shortcut record SETS coincide on the verified range

The analysis below uses all 148 known records to 1.47e19 (Roosendaal's

## 2. The mechanism behind 4/3 — dynasty links

If n ≡ 1 (mod 3), then m = (4n−1)/3 is an odd integer whose full-map
trajectory is m → 4n → 2n → n, so

    delay(m) = delay(n) + 3,   m/n = 4/3 − 1/(3n).

A record breaker with such an heir often hands the crown straight to it:
**33 of the 147 consecutive record pairs are EXACTLY R_{k+1} = (4R_k−1)/3**
(share by thirds of the list: 16% → 14% → 37%, rising). Another 16 pairs are
exact doublings 2R_k (delay +1), and 9 more have R_{k+1}'s trajectory
passing exactly through R_k at distances 6–106 steps.

The ratio distribution is therefore a mixture:
- an **atom at 4/3 − 1/(3R_k) → 4/3** (the four-thirds heirs),
- an atom at 2 (doublings; frequency decaying),
- a spread from unrelated links ("revolutions").

Median over all 147 ratios: **1.333333**; mean 1.377 (the mean does NOT
tend to 4/3; extreme ratios up to 2 persist).

## 3. Rivers, not points: the merge structure

For each consecutive pair, intersect the two full trajectories:
- median shared-river fraction (fraction of R_{k+1}'s trajectory that
  coincides with R_k's) is **0.93 overall and 0.99 in the last third**;
- the typical new champion joins the old river a median of **8 steps**
  below the old source: records mostly RE-USE the previous record's entire
  descent and only re-plumb the headwaters;
- but punctuating these dynastic stretches are near-total reroutes
  (shared fraction 0.4%–6%), e.g. k=121, 124, 143–144, still present at
  n ~ 10^19.

## 4. Verdict on the conjecture (our reading)

- **Median → 4/3: well supported.** The exact-atom pins the median; its
  share is the largest single component and rising.
- **Convergence in probability: not supported by the present data.** The
  non-atom links' |log(ratio · 3/4)| has median 0.10 / 0.13 / 0.18 by
  thirds — drifting AWAY from 4/3, not toward it. In-probability
  convergence therefore requires the atom share → 1, for which the
  evidence (16/14/37%) is suggestive but weak.
- Refined conjecture we'd offer the poster: **the four-thirds-heir share
  p_k tends to a limit p ∈ (1/2, 1]; the ratios converge in distribution
  to (1−p)·μ + p·δ_{4/3}** with μ a nontrivial spread. Median → 4/3 iff
  p > 1/2 asymptotically (equivalently: the "revolutions" never die out —
  they visibly persist to 1.47e19 — but stay a minority).
- Note the per-delay economics: a four-thirds heir buys 3 delay for
  log(4/3) of log-height — (4/3)^{1/3} ≈ 1.1006 per delay unit — the
  cheapest known regular move; doubling pays 2 per single delay unit.
  This is the structural reason the atom dominates the frontier.
