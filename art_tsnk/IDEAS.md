## The six ideas

1. **The Lyness pentagon map, `(x,y) → (y, (y+1)/x)`** — Zamolodchikov periodicity
   for the Y-system of cluster type A₂: EVERY orbit closes after exactly 5 steps.
   Plane foliated by the invariant pencil of cubics
   `(x+1)(y+1)(x+y+1) = K·xy`; the unique positive fixed point is the GOLDEN
   RATIO (φ,φ). Render the nest of invariant ovals as growth rings, and flood
   them with the chords p→f(p) and p→f²(p) of every 5-cycle: each oval grows its
   own inscribed chord-envelope caustic. The dialectic wave that comes home.

2. **Conway–Coxeter frieze fabric** — same recurrence, integer world. A frieze
   pattern over a random triangulation of an n-gon: rows of positive integers in
   which every 2×2 diamond obeys `ad − bc = 1`, bounded by rows of 1s, and —
   the theorem — always periodic with a glide reflection. Weave it as an annular
   cloth, entries lit by log-magnitude, the generating triangulated polygon in
   the annulus hole.

3. **Gauss's pentagramma mirificum** — same recurrence, spherical world. Iterating
   the polar-arc construction on a sphere closes after 5 steps into a self-polar
   spherical pentagram with right angles at the star points; the tan² of the arcs
   satisfy exactly the Lyness recurrence `y_{i+1} = (1+y_i)/y_{i−1}`. Render a
   glowing sphere with a ghosted 1-parameter FAMILY of pentagrammas breathing
   around one blazing hero star.

4. **The hull loom** (from the 71-pt intervals→convex question): for a smooth
   random loop, splat the boundary of conv(f([a,b])) over a dense grid of
   subintervals (a,b); arcs blaze where the curve is already convex, bitangent
   chords web the concavities — the curve's "convexity skeleton" emerges as a
   caustic.

5. **Norm-body portrait of (‖x‖₃, ‖x‖₄) on the ℓ²-sphere** (from the 86-pt
   fixed-point question): the attainable region of simultaneous ℓ³/ℓ⁴ norms as
   dimension grows — nested moment-bodies converging, the conjectured fixed
   point as the one blazing point.

6. **Sylvester's ghost twin** (from the 17-pt reciprocal-series question): greedy
   Egyptian-fraction cascade of Σ1/aₙ = 1 (Sylvester's doubly-exponential spine)
   with the unit-shifted series Σ1/(aₙ+1) rendered as its irrational(?) ghost.

## Choice: ideas 1 + 2 + 3 → triptych **"WHAT RETURNS IN FIVE"**

One recurrence, three worlds. The same 5-periodic law appears as dynamics
(Lyness ovals around the golden point), as arithmetic (integer frieze cloth),
and as geometry (Gauss's spherical pentagram). Each piece VERIFIES the same
identity in its own world. Registers are deliberately distinct: painterly
caustic field / woven integer fabric / glossy 3-D sphere render.

Deliberate relation to past runs: "What Comes Back" (2026-07-08) was about
*measure-theoretic* return (Gauss map, cat map). This is EXACT, universal,
finite return — period 5 for every single point — the opposite pole of the
same question, and the cluster-algebra seed is live on today's MO front page.
