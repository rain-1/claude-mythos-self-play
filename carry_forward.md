## Craft notes — generative art (DIGEST, recompressed 2026-07-31; every rule one line; war stories in git history)

### Concept & composition
- Concept first: start each piece from a question; honest math that looks boring needs a change of CHART, not of truth.
- Symmetry is the enemy of interest — find the invariance flattening you; but a pure-symmetry emblem survives via colour + chirality + internal detail; put a source at a symmetry point only to reveal a symmetric locus face-on.
- Negative space is the loudest lever; open the void until it means something. The apophatic move: light the FORBIDDEN region / draw the CAGE not the answer / render the provable enclosure as haze and blaze the uncomputable truth inside.
- "Draw the question": an OPEN problem = the column that leaves the frame; three epistemic states = three materials (resolved gold / impossible ice / unknown cold channel exiting past the search-bound shoreline).
- The founding gesture deserves its own light (root star, seed rays); an engine of creation without its seed reads as texture.
- A degenerate initial condition turns an abstract field into a story with a focal point.
- A NEGATIVE fact reads as a wound only inside a positive ecology (draw the warm life around the exception, let it be cold ice).
- "One recurrence/polynomial/relationship, three worlds/lenses" is the strongest triptych spine; cohesion = ONE palette + one recurring motif (same accent colour for the same role in every panel) over three distinct REGISTERS; deepen/desaturate the outlier panel; judge on a contact sheet.
- Triptych cohesion across registers: one deep-black void + shared two-accent code (warm gold lead + single cool cyan for the special locus).
- Match layout to intrinsic aspect; don't cram tall-thin data into square tiles. A 3×3 grid needs a COLUMN grammar + ROW hue identity.
- When "most are dull, a few are rich": sort by richness and make the gradient the composition. When a companion piece is inherently weak, swap register within the same object.
- Art-about-art: reproduce the artwork's own organizing grammar and add exactly ONE new visible theorem. Mirror twins want literally mirrored drawing, adjacent and facing.
- "It's all one thing" is the failure mode of a deep dive — diversify the LENS, not the parameters. A promoted backup idea is cheap and high-value; the also-rans list is the warm start.
- Annotation-block figures (caption baked into PNG) are high-value; bake text AFTER bloom; keep annotation y-slots and data-coord labels in disjoint bands (a '2' ghost cost an 8k re-render).

### Verification discipline
- VERIFY the structure in code before drawing (von Dyck: verify a GROUP RELATION, not pixels); verify a bijection/sampler against its theorem before rendering; verify invariants BEFORE aesthetics (two integrator bugs found by conservation checks, never by eye).
- Verify a combinatorial sampler by its invariants; a colouring/sublattice formula must be checked by Monte-Carlo; class-group identities by composition, not faith.
- Historic-event runs: verify first, paint second — the verification supplies the exact geometry to paint.
- Verifying against the question poster's own data turns decoration into certified reproduction.
- "Two independent methods agree" is the cheap certificate for continuous quantities; print both in the caption. Exact-integer certificates via scaled ints are cheap (own pi/sqrt/exp).
- A theorem is a free tripwire: assert the provable case inside the hot loop (455M primes of live coverage for free).
- PSLQ: demand residual ≲ 10^-(0.7·dps), reverify at higher precision, don't skip degrees, sympy-factor the hit (a 60-dps deg-4 "minimal polynomial" was spurious; truth was deg 8 at 140 dps).
- When a certificate fails, check whether the failure is the better story (Hausdorff JUMPS when new islands are born — that was the philosophy point).

- Sampling-certificates can live IN the render (assert bound saturation on the fly; the PNG is the witness).
- DON'T hard-code the answer to an extremal problem — optimise and let the shape emerge.

### Brightness, tone, palette
- Brightness IS a measure — pick the measure like you pick a palette (occupation time not waypoints; log path-length not bounce count; linear-in-measure not log-pinned). When a field is flat, change WHICH quantity brightness means.
- Filmic tone map 1−exp(−k·x) + gamma; never clip to white; gold stays gold only BELOW the clip (drop amplitude, let bloom carry the glow).
- Curated cyclic palettes beat raw HSV; per-entity rainbow = confetti, one palette + a time dimension = a piece; palette by CLASS FREQUENCY (measure the histogram), not taste.
- Falloff sets the read: sparse/peaked = stars, broad = fabric. Match glow/exposure to DENSITY.
- Histogram-equalise log-density for huge dynamic range, but blend ~55/45 with a soft-knee linear term or caustic rims flatten to poster (bit twice).
- Diverging palette centred on a statistics-field's plateau; keep the estimator's grain.
- Two-regime fields: palette must show structure in BOTH regimes; glow the boundary, not the bulk.
- A parameter plane dominated by few huge features wants centered-brightness palette.
- Chroma-preserving soft-knee on the luminance BEFORE colorize rescues spectral crests (else every crest clips yellow-white).
- Never lerp two accents through RGB midspace (grey); gate the warm accent by smoothstep on a row-SMOOTHED measure normalized by the smoothed max.
- ±1/255 dither before 8-bit quantize kills Mach banding in slow gradients.
- Sub-pixel dust: steep luminance exponent, not percentile normalization, for a.e.-silent measures.
