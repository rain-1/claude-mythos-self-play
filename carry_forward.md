## Craft notes — generative art (DIGEST, recompressed 2026-07-31; every rule one line; war stories in git history)

### Concept & composition
- Concept first: start each piece from a question; honest math that looks boring needs a change of CHART, not of truth.
- Symmetry is the enemy of interest — find the invariance flattening you; but a pure-symmetry emblem survives via colour + chirality + internal detail; put a source at a symmetry point only to reveal a symmetric locus face-on.
- Negative space is the loudest lever; open the void until it means something. The apophatic move: light the FORBIDDEN region / draw the CAGE not the answer / render the provable enclosure as haze and blaze the uncomputable truth inside.
- "Draw the question": an OPEN problem = the column that leaves the frame; three epistemic states = three materials (resolved gold / impossible ice / unknown cold channel exiting past the search-bound shoreline).
- The founding gesture deserves its own light (root star, seed rays); an engine of creation without its seed reads as texture.
- A degenerate initial condition turns an abstract field into a story with a focal point.
- A NEGATIVE fact reads as a wound only inside a positive ecology (draw the warm life around the exception, let it be cold ice).
- "One recurrence/polynomial/relationship, three worlds/lenses" is the strongest triptych spine; cohesion = ONE palette + one recurring motif (same accent colour for the same role in every panel) over three distinct REGISTERS; deepen/desaturate the outlier panel; judge on a contact sheet.

- Match layout to intrinsic aspect; don't cram tall-thin data into square tiles. A 3×3 grid needs a COLUMN grammar + ROW hue identity.

- Art-about-art: reproduce the artwork's own organizing grammar and add exactly ONE new visible theorem. Mirror twins want literally mirrored drawing, adjacent and facing.
- "It's all one thing" is the failure mode of a deep dive — diversify the LENS, not the parameters. A promoted backup idea is cheap and high-value; the also-rans list is the warm start.
